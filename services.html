Services We Offer

Comprehensive BIM Solutions
for Every Project Stage

From concept to construction, we provide end-to-end BIM services tailored to meet the unique needs of architects, engineers, contractors and developers across multiple industries.

Services we offer
Sl. No. Service Category Scope of Work Enquire
01 Structural BIM Core BIM Accurate structural modelling and detailing of foundations, columns, beams, slabs and steel for safe, buildable structures. Enquire
02 Architectural BIM Core BIM Detailed architectural models covering walls, doors, windows, finishes and interiors for better design and visualization. Enquire
03 HVAC BIM MEPF BIM Ductwork, chillers, AHUs, diffusers and HVAC piping modelled and coordinated with all trades. Enquire
04 Electrical BIM MEPF BIM Cable trays, conduits, panels, lighting and power layouts with load-ready model data. Enquire
05 ELV BIM MEPF BIM Extra-low-voltage systems: CCTV, access control, data networks, fire alarm and public address. Enquire
06 Plumbing BIM MEPF BIM Water supply, drainage, sanitary and storm-water networks with correct slopes and fixtures. Enquire
07 Fire Fighting BIM MEPF BIM Sprinkler, hydrant and fire-suppression systems modelled to code with pump-room layouts. Enquire
08 Clash Co-ordination Coordination Multi-discipline clash detection and resolution reports to eliminate conflicts before construction. Enquire
09 Sheet Preparation Deliverables Coordinated plans, sections, elevations and shop-drawing sheets set up to project title-block standards. Enquire
10 BIM Documentation Deliverables Schedules, quantity take-offs, as-built records and handover documentation for the full project lifecycle. Enquire
11 3D Modelling Deliverables Data-rich 3D models at the required LOD for design review, analysis and construction. Enquire
12 2D Drawings Deliverables Precise 2D CAD drafting, CAD-to-BIM conversion and construction drawings. Enquire
