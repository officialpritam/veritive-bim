Services We Offer

Comprehensive BIM Solutions
for Every Project Stage

From concept to construction, we provide end-to-end BIM services tailored to meet the unique needs of architects, engineers, contractors and developers across multiple industries.

Services we offer
Sl. No. Service Category Scope of Work Enquire
01 2D Drafting Drafting Precise 2D CAD drafting of plans, sections, elevations and details. Enquire
02 Architectural 3D Model Core BIM Detailed 3D models of walls, doors, windows, finishes and interiors for design review and coordination. Enquire
03 Structural 3D Model Core BIM Accurate 3D models of foundations, columns, beams, slabs and steel for buildable structures. Enquire
04 Mechanical 3D Model MEPF 3D Model Ductwork, chillers, AHUs, diffusers and HVAC piping modelled and coordinated with all trades. Enquire
05 Electrical 3D Model MEPF 3D Model Cable trays, conduits, panels, lighting and power layouts modelled in 3D. Enquire
06 Plumbing 3D Model MEPF 3D Model Water supply, drainage, sanitary and storm-water networks with correct slopes and fixtures. Enquire
07 Fire Fighting 3D Model MEPF 3D Model Sprinkler, hydrant and fire-suppression systems modelled with pump-room layouts. Enquire
08 MEPF Clash Coordination Coordination Multi-discipline clash detection and resolution reports to remove conflicts before construction. Enquire
09 Shop Drawing / Installation Drawing Deliverables Coordinated shop and installation drawings for fabrication and on-site construction. Enquire
10 BIM Documentation Deliverables Schedules, quantity take-offs, as-built records and handover documentation. Enquire
11 Scan to BIM Reality Capture Point-cloud data from laser scans converted into accurate as-built BIM models. Enquire
12 BIM Staffing Staffing Skilled BIM modellers and coordinators to extend your project team. Enquire
