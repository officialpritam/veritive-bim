About Vertive

Engineering Precision Through BIM

VERTIVE BIM ENGINEERING SERVICES is a dedicated BIM and engineering solutions provider, delivering high-quality models, drawings and coordination support for architecture, structure and MEP disciplines. We combine technical expertise with modern BIM workflows to help clients build smarter and faster.

2D Drafting

Precise 2D CAD drafting of plans, sections, elevations and details.

Architectural 3D Model

Detailed architectural models for better design, visualization and execution.

Structural 3D Model

Accurate structural models and detailing for safe, efficient and buildable structures.

MEPF 3D Model

Mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire fighting systems modelled in 3D.

MEPF Clash Coordination

Multi-discipline clash detection and resolution before construction begins.

Shop Drawing / Installation Drawing

Coordinated shop and installation drawings for fabrication and site work.

BIM Documentation

Schedules, quantities, as-built records and handover documentation.

Scan to BIM

Point-cloud data from laser scans converted into as-built BIM models.

BIM Staffing

Skilled BIM modellers and coordinators to extend your project team.
