01Introduction

VERTIVE BIM ENGINEERING SERVICES ("VERTIVE", "we", "us" or "our") is a BIM and engineering services provider based in Haldia, West Bengal, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, contact us, engage us for a project or apply for a job with us.

We process personal data in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. By using our website or sharing information with us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, phone number, the service or subject you select and the content of your message when you use our contact forms or email us.
  • Job applications: your name, contact details, experience level, resume/CV, cover note and any other details you choose to include.
  • Project engagements: business contact details, billing information and project materials such as drawings, specifications, models and site data.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting server records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the date and time of access. We use this only to keep the website secure and working properly.

03How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare proposals and quotations.
  • To plan, deliver and support the BIM and engineering services you engage us for.
  • To assess job applications and contact candidates about suitable roles.
  • To send invoices, maintain business records and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • To maintain the security and performance of our website and prevent misuse.

We process your information on the basis of the consent you give when you submit it, or where it is necessary to perform a contract with you or comply with the law. We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we will not send you marketing messages unless you have asked to receive them.

04Client Project Data

Drawings, models, specifications and other project materials you share with us are treated as confidential. We use them only to deliver the agreed scope of work, restrict access to the team members working on your project and do not share them with third parties without your written permission. Where a separate Non-Disclosure Agreement or service contract applies, its confidentiality terms take precedence over this section.

05Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • With trusted service providers who help us operate our business, such as web hosting, email and cloud storage providers, under obligations to keep it confidential and secure.
  • With professional advisers such as accountants and legal counsel where necessary.
  • When required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect our rights, property or safety and that of others.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition or transfer of our business, in which case the recipient will be bound by this policy.

06Cookies & Third-Party Services

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To display the site, your browser loads some content from third-party providers, which may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts for typography.
  • Google Maps for the office location map on our Contact page.
  • jsDelivr for icon and animation libraries.
  • Unsplash for some photographs.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The website will continue to work, although the embedded map may not display.

07Data Retention

  • General enquiries are kept for up to 24 months, unless they lead to a project.
  • Job applications are kept for up to 12 months so we can consider you for future openings, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Client and project records are kept for the duration of the engagement and afterwards for as long as required by law or reasonably needed for warranty, support and dispute purposes.

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

08Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access controls, secure storage and confidentiality obligations for our team. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a personal data breach where the law requires it.

09Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Request a summary of the personal information we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to erase your information where it is no longer needed.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at query@vertivebim.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

10International Transfers

We work with clients in several countries, and some of our service providers may store data outside India. Where information is transferred abroad, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected to the standard described in this policy and as permitted under Indian law.

11Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website.

13Contact & Grievances
